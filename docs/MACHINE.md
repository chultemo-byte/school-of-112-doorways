## Research braid (do not collapse into one fake canon)

1. *Haṭhābhyāsapaddhati* — only premodern text that *describes* 112 āsanas, in six groups: supine 1–22, prone 23–47, stationary 48–74, standing 75–93, rope 94–103, sun-moon piercing 104–112. Influenced *Śrītattvanidhi* and likely Krishnamacharya.
2. *Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra* — Śiva gives Devī 112 *dhāraṇās*. Inner methods. Companion meditation per door, never a replacement for the asana.
3. Shaiva / Isha 114-chakra map — 72,000 nāḍīs meet at 114 junctions. Two outside the body. 112 inside. Of those, 108 can be worked; 4 flower as consequence. The 112 arrange as **7 dimensions × 16 aspects**. Those 7 dimensions are the basis of seven schools of yoga.
4. Classical Haṭha — Śiva selected 84 from 8,400,000 living forms. Principal current inside our 112.
5. Patañjali Yoga Sūtra 2.46 — *sthira-sukham āsanam*. Pass/fail for every generated lesson.
