Tejas Yātrī

The water

Breath is four events, not two. Counts are banks. The turns are the door.

The four events

  1. Inhale — mist climbs. The diaphragm drops. The ribs can widen.
  2. Top turn — a natural hitch. Notice. Do not squeeze.
  3. Exhale — mist falls. The diaphragm rises. Weight arrives.
  4. Bottom turn — the next inhale is born. This is start.

Start on the exhale. The mountain is a settling shape. Inhale is rise. Exhale is standing.

Red light

If the pose steals the breath, the pose is too much. Locked knees hide the exhale in the chest. Kids never hold. A held breath from strain is not kumbhaka. It is a stop.

A longer exhale often quiets the system. That is a nervous-system shift, not medicine.
