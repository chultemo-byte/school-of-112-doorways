The water

Four events. One start.

Start on the exhale. Mist at the feet. Then inhale — mist climbs. Then exhale — mist falls. Six loops. No forced hold.

1. Inhale volume rises. Diaphragm drops. Crown can lift a finger-width. Do not shrug.

2. Top turn a hitch that is already there. Notice it. Do not squeeze it into a contest.

3. Exhale volume falls. Weight into the look-here. This is arrival.

4. Bottom turn the next inhale is born here. Rest one pulse. Then let it rise.

Counts are banks, not the river

A count is a pulse you can keep without gasping. Not a clock second. Not a frequency.

Where it lands

If you only count, you are still on the bank. The upgrade is a landing: soles, hinge, sacrum, long back, low belly, standing foot. One place. Not everywhere.

Red light

Breath that stops because the pose is fighting you is not kumbhaka. Soften the knees. Sit. Kids never hold. A chair still drinks the water.

Honest chemistry: a longer exhale often quiets the system. If it does not quiet you, you did not fail the door.
