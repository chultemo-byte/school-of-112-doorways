The exit
Exhale the arms down to Mountain. Soften the shoulders.
Yes
Shoulders away from the ears. Soft knees.
No
Arms jammed behind the ears.

The water

Inhale arms up for four. Soft pause at the top. Exhale four. Never force the pause. Mist climbs in. Mist falls out.

Geometry

Mountain base. Arms rise on one line from soles through side ribs to fingertips. Soft elbows. Shoulders drop. Ribs quiet. Do not tip back.

Five ways

1 Stand. 2 Raise both arms. 3 Drop the shoulders. 4 Breathe four and four. 5 Stop if it hurts.

Safety

If the shoulders or low back complain, lower the arms. A chair still opens this door. Practice is not medicine.
