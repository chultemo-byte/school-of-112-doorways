Mountain

Tāḍāsana · the axis · grown-up name, same shape

Stand like you mean it. Not like a soldier. Like a mountain that has no appointment.

Look hereThe three points under your feet: heel, big-toe pad, little-toe pad. Press each one gently. The one that goes quiet is where your mind wandered. That is the door opening — not the pose getting prettier.

Smell a flower — four counts. Blow a bubble — four counts. Six rounds. Soft knees the whole time.

Green light: weight even, knees soft, breath easy, you can name which foot point answered.
Red light: locked knees, ribs flared, shoulders at your ears, breath held. Soften or sit. Still the door.

One question before you leave: which foot point went quiet first?
