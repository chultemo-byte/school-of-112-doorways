Geometry

A vertical column from the soles through the pelvis and ribs to the crown. Base triangle: two feet and the pelvic floor. Feet hip-width or together. Arms soft at the sides. Zero-degree tilt.

Orientation

Crown over heels. Weight even on both feet. Gaze soft and level. Gravity down through the three foot points. Length up through the crown. Face any quiet wall; the mountain does not chase a compass.

Physics

Three-point base resists sway. Soft knees keep the line alive. A locked knee is a held breath in the leg.

Reaction

School metaphor only: Settle. Scattered charge moves toward grounded stillness. Not a chemical or medical claim.

Breath

4 in, 0 hold, 4 out, 6 cycles. Lands in the lower belly, then soft ribs. Notice the natural turn at the top and bottom. Do not stop the breath.

Attention

Primary: soles-to-crown line. Secondary: the two breath-turns. House focus: Mūlādhāra — the floor under the feet.

Philosophy

Sthira-sukham āsanam. The mountain does not imagine. It just stands. Door 001 is paired with VBT dharana 001 as a school device, not ancient one-to-one canon.

Mentality

Nothing to prove. Arrive. If the mind wanders, return to the quiet foot point.

Emotion

Quiet pride is allowed. Fidgeting is information, not failure.

Benefits

Wakes the feet. Settles fidgeting. Gives the school its axis. A chair still opens this door.

Five ways

1 Stand. 2 Soften the knees. 3 Find three points. 4 Breathe 4 and 4. 5 Stop if it hurts.

Eight beats

1 Name the door. 2 Stack the line. 3 Crown over heels. 4 Four and four. 5 Hold the axis. 6 Name Settle. 7 Rest at the breath-turns. 8 Soften and carry the quiet.

Safety

Pain is information. Never force. Soften the knees if locking bothers them. Sit or hold a wall if balance fades. Practice is not medicine.
