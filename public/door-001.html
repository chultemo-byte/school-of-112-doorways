Adiyogi

VBT dharana 001 (verse 24)

In standing stillness, follow natural breath without forcing. At the end of inhale and the end of exhale, rest attention for a moment in the brief turn (inner center and outer dvadasanta as classical map). Do not strain or stop the breath; notice the pause that is already there.

Safety

  1. Pain is information. Pause, regress, or exit. Never push through sharp pain.
  2. If standing is not available, practice the same breath-turn attention seated upright in an open seat (sukhasana or chair).
  3. General practice caution only; not medical advice. Soften the knees if locking bothers them. Sit or hold a wall if balance or fatigue appear.

Five ways

  1. This is Door 001, Mountain — Tadasana. Stand as a quiet upright column. Nothing to prove. Just arrive.
  2. Feet plant. Legs long, knees soft. Pelvis stacked over ankles, ribs over pelvis, crown floating up. Arms rest by your sides. One vertical line.
  3. Crown over heels. Weight even on both feet — heel, big-toe ball, little-toe ball. Gaze soft and level. Gravity down, length up.
  4. Inhale for four. Exhale for four. Six easy cycles. Breath lands in the lower belly, then soft ribs. No forced hold.
  5. Take one free breath. Soften the knees. Carry the vertical quiet into the next step, or sit if you need rest.
