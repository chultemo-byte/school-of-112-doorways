The School of 112 Doorways

The School is living memory. Each door is one dhāraṇā of the Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra, a way of resting attention that Bhairava describes to the Goddess. Door N is dhāraṇā N in Jaideva Singh's numbering, so Door 001 is verse 24.

House 1 is open: doors 001 to 016. Four of them (004, 008, 013, 014) are historical and observe only. They are kept as classical descriptions to read, not practised.
