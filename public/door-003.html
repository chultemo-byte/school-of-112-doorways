Look here

Eyes soft toward shins or closed; neck hangs free.

Water

Inhale for four to lengthen the back of the body; exhale for six as the fold settles. No forced breath-hold. Soften on the longer out-breath.

Geometry

Polygon. inverted V / hanging triangle

From standing, feet about hip-width, tip the pelvis at the hip crease and fold the torso toward the legs. Keep knees soft as needed. Hands rest on floor, blocks, shins, or ankles. Crown hangs toward the floor; neck soft. The shape is an inverted V: feet as base points, hips as apex, spine and head as the hanging line.

Safety

  1. Pain is information. Pause, regress, or exit. Never push through sharp pain.
  2. Bend knees generously, hands on blocks or shins, or take Ardha Uttanasana (half fold with long spine) if the full hang strains the low back or hamstrings.
  3. General practice caution only; not medical advice. Soften or exit if sharp low-back, hamstring, or head pressure appears. Avoid forcing straight legs. Use props. If dizziness arises when inverted, come up slowly with a long spine or bent knees.

Five ways

  1. This is Door D-003: Standing Forward Fold, Uttanasana. A standing fold that hangs the crown toward the floor.
  2. Feet hip-width. Tip from the hip crease into an inverted V: hips high, spine hanging, hands to floor, blocks, or shins. Soft knees are welcome.
  3. Hinge at the hips, not the waist. Let the crown yield to gravity. Keep weight through the feet; do not yank yourself deeper.
  4. Inhale four to lengthen the back body. Exhale six to settle the fold. Six easy cycles. No forced holds.
  5. Bend the knees. Rise slowly with a long spine, or step back to a milder fold. Stand and feel the after-quiet before the next door.
