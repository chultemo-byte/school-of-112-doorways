Door 008 · House 1 · Mūlādhāra

Filling to the Crown, Crossing the Brow-Bridge

Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra · dhāraṇā 8 · verse 31 (Jaideva Singh numbering)

Door 8Verse 31visualization · breath · kuṇḍalinī / subtle bodyHistorical / observe onlyDraft · awaiting guardian review
Historical / observe onlyThis dhāraṇā is kept as a classical description to read and understand. It is not practised here, and no practice cues are given. Forceful breath-filling, breath suspension and sustained brow contraction are not taught by this school. They can bring headache, dizziness or light-headedness. Do not attempt them from a written description.

Observe only: read the verse and its classical description. This door is not practised.

The verse

tayāpūryāśu mūrdhāntaṃ bhaṅktvā bhrūkṣepasetunā | nirvikalpaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā sarvordhve sarvagodgamaḥ ||

Having quickly filled [the body] up to the top of the head with that [breath-power], and having broken through by means of the 'bridge' made by contracting the eyebrows, make the mind free of thought-constructs; then, in what is above all, the all-pervading arises.

School paraphrase drawing on Jaideva Singh (1979), Christopher Wallis and Bettina Bäumer; not a quotation.

Commentary

Singh: fill the mūrdhānta with prāṇic energy and cross it with the bridge-like contraction of the eyebrows. Singh adds that the esoteric bhrūkṣepa technique 'was a closely guarded secret among the mystics and is now practically lost'. The Mystery Within's own rendering reads the 'breaking' as suspending the breath while concentrating at the ājñā cakra.

Classical description

Classical method: rapidly filling the body to the crown with breath-power, breaking through with a contraction of the eyebrows, and making the mind free of thought.

Historical / observe only. Read and understood, not practised. No practice cues are given for this door.

Safety

  • Pain is information. Pause, regress, or exit. Never push through sharp pain.
  • General practice caution only; not medical advice. Forceful breath-filling, breath suspension and sustained brow contraction are not taught by this school. They can bring headache, dizziness or light-headedness. Do not attempt them from a written description. If anything feels distressing (dizziness, agitation, strong emotion), stop, open the eyes and rest attention on the feet and the floor.
