Look here

Eyes lowered, or gently closed. No fixed stare. If the eyes close and the mind spins, leave them softly open toward the floor.

Water

Inhale 4, exhale 6, pause 2 after exhale. None in this teaching script. Mahā bandha is outside the door until a living teacher gives it in person. Do not add locks to make the seat feel real.

Geometry

Polygon. octagon

Horizontal plane. Eight points of a regular octagon, a teaching figure for a many-petaled seat. Not a diagram of how far a knee must travel.

Vertices are on the record.

Reaction

Phase. dissolution

Reactants. legs that argue; senses leaning outward

Catalyst. hips that already know external rotation, and time

Product. a seat that can be left as easily as it was taken

Metaphor only. If a knee speaks, there is no product to force. The reaction does not run.

Orientation

Gaze. Eyes lowered, or gently closed. No fixed stare. If the eyes close and the mind spins, leave them softly open toward the floor.

Hands. Only if the legs have found the seat without force: hands rest on the knees, palms soft, thumb and index finger lightly joined. If the seat is not available, the same hands rest this way on the knees in a chair or in an easy cross-legged seat. The hand shape does not require lotus.

Joint lead. The hips. The knees are followers. If a knee must be persuaded, the door is shut. Ankles are not handles.

Attention

the center of the chest, quieter than the shape of the legs

Adiyogi

Verse 138

Teaching reference, marked historical and observe_only: not a class drill, and no readiness flag promotes it into one. Verse 138 is yukti 112 in the usual counting of the Vijnana Bhairava Tantra, the last of the 112 brief instructions. It points toward a settling in which restless mind, breath-as-effort, the feeling of being a separate knower, and the self-image are no longer being held in place. This gloss is not a translation and not a technique for forcing that settling. Verse 49's heart-lotus is a cousin image; this door does not collapse the two into one claim. The pose is never the method.

Guardian

PADMA

Safety

  1. Never force Padmāsana. Knee cartilage does not negotiate. If either knee lifts high, complains, or the foot must be hauled into place, come out at once.
  2. Prior knee injury, knee surgery, ligament sprain, or pain under the kneecap: do not attempt lotus. A chair, or an easy seat in which both knees are comfortable, is the door for that day.
  3. Ankle sprain, Achilles pain, or hips that do not already externally rotate: same rule. The hips lead. The ankles are not levers.
  4. Hip replacement, or any limit a physician has set on hip rotation: stay inside that limit. This text is not a clearance to fold the leg.
  5. Pregnancy: lotus is optional and often uncomfortable. Leave for numbness, pubic pain, or breath that cannot deepen. Side-sitting or a bolster is enough.
  6. Sciatica or pain that travels down the leg: do not fold the legs into a shape that reproduces it.
  7. Numbness in a foot is a reason to leave immediately, not a sign of progress.

Five ways

  1. Show the shape on a drawing, or on a teacher whose hips already have it. The student does not copy with the hands.
  2. Teach the same hands, gaze, and breath in a chair, and say plainly that the chair is an honest seat for today.
  3. If the student is close, one foot may rest on the opposite thigh only when that knee descends without being pressed. Full lotus is not the homework.
  4. Keep the breath in whatever seat is already available. If the breath shortens in order to win the shape, abandon the shape.
  5. Read verse 138 aloud as study. Do not ask the student to make mind, breath, knowing, or self-image dissolve. Close by feeling the feet, or the chair, ordinary and awake.

Exit

Never force Padmāsana. Knee cartilage does not negotiate. If either knee lifts high, complains, or the foot must be hauled into place, come out at once.
