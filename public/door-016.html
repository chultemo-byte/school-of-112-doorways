Safety

  1. Pain is information. Pause, regress, or exit. Never push through sharp pain.
  2. Foot to calf or toes to floor; stand near a wall; or keep both feet down in Mountain with arms branching up.
  3. Avoid pressing the raised foot into the standing knee joint. Use wall or lower foot placement if balance is unstable. General practice caution only; not medical advice.

Five ways

  1. This is Door 016: Tree, Vrksasana. One standing root, one open branch, tall spine.
  2. From Mountain, shift onto one foot. Place the other foot on the inner thigh or calf—never the knee. Hands at heart or reach up.
  3. Crown over the standing heel. Keep the pelvis square. Gaze soft and fixed ahead. Wall is welcome.
  4. Breathe four counts in, four out, about six rounds. Let the exhale land in the standing foot and low belly.
  5. Lower the foot with control. Stand in Mountain. Switch sides when ready. Carry the quiet root into your next step.
