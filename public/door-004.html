Water

Inhale to lift into half fold and lengthen. Softly notice the brief pause at the top without forcing retention. Exhale to return to fold or step forward as cued.

Geometry

Polygon. Inverted L: vertical leg column + horizontal torso line (table spine)

From a forward fold, lift the torso until the back is long and roughly parallel to the floor. Hands rest on shins, thighs, blocks, or the floor—whichever height keeps the spine long. Legs stay rooted; knees may soft-bend. The shape is a hinge at the hips with a flat, extended back body, not a deeper fold.

Safe class form: in the half-lift, lengthen on the inhale, then briefly notice the natural still point at the end of the breath—inside after inhale or outside after exhale—without forcing a hard kumbhaka. Use the pause as a door of attention, then continue with soft equal breath.

Safety

  1. Pain is information. Pause, regress, or exit. Never push through sharp pain.
  2. Hands on thighs, blocks, chair seat, or wall; soft bend in knees. Flat long spine beats straight legs or floor touch.
  3. General practice caution only; not medical advice. Avoid pressing hands on the knee joints. Ease out if low-back, hamstring, or neck sensation turns sharp. Pregnancy and back concerns: use a higher hand support and keep the spine long rather than deep.

Five ways

  1. This door is Half Forward Fold, Ardha Uttanasana. It is the halfway lift—a long spine pause inside the fold, not a deeper dive.
  2. Hinge at the hips. Lift the torso until the back is long and roughly parallel to the floor. Hands find shins, thighs, or blocks—whatever keeps that long line.
  3. Sit bones reach back; crown reaches forward. Gaze soft to the floor ahead. Neck stays in line with the spine. Weight stays even in both feet.
  4. Inhale four counts to lift and lengthen. Soft hold for two counts at the top. Exhale four counts to fold or step on. Six easy cycles.
  5. Exhale back to fold, or inhale to rise. Soften the knees. Carry the long-spine cue into the next standing shape. Pain means pause or exit.
