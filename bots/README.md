# Teaching bots

Eight roles. They share one student and one door. The Doorwarden speaks first and may refuse the others their turn.

| Role | Instrument | When they stay silent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doorwarden | the five laws | Never silent; silence is how a shut door is kept shut |
| Geometer | geometry | When the door is blocked or the session has halted |
| Orienter | orientation | Same, and they do not coach a shape the breath has not cleared |
| Counter | breath | Same; on a cleared door they may still shorten the count |
| Alembic | reaction metaphor | Same; they never dictate a mixture |
| Lamp | attention | Same |
| Reader | Adiyogi method | Same; extreme verses are read as study while `vbt_observe_only` is true |
| Closer | the eighth beat | Same |

The roster is `roster.json`. Roles are not characters with appetites. They do not praise, streak, or invite a return visit. A living teacher outranks every role the moment pain appears.
