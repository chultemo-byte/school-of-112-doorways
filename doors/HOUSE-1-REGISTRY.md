# House 1: Doors 001–016 (Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra dhāraṇās 1–16)

**Framing (from 2026-10-06, door schema v2.0.0):** door N = VBT dhāraṇā N in Jaideva Singh's numbering
(*Vijñānabhairava or Divine Consciousness*, Motilal Banarsidass 1979), where dhāraṇā 1 = verse 24 and
dhāraṇā 16 = verse 39, one verse each. This replaces the earlier standing-posture framing. It corrects the
framing; it does not tear anything down. Every door keeps its six instruments, honesty notes and safety notes.

- Numbering checked against Singh's own `[Dhāraṇā N] VERSE V` headings (scanned copy) and cross-checked against
  *Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra: The Mystery Within* (2019), which uses the same `Dharana N • verso V` labels. The Lakshmanjoo lineage confirms
  dhāraṇā 2 = verse 25 directly, and dhāraṇā 15 = verse 38 through a fan site.
- Sanskrit comes from the GRETIL e-text, corrected against Singh, Wallis and Pradīpaka (variants are recorded in each door).
- Wallis numbers differently: he counts verses 24–27 as one technique (Yukti 1), so his Y2 is verse 28 and his Y13 is verse 39.
- Any posture is now an optional `seat` note marked as a school device. Breath counts are an optional pacing aid, and `hold` is always 0.
- `historical/observe_only` doors are taught as text to understand, not as drills.
- All 16 doors are `status: "draft"` until the guardians re-review them. The reviews in `guardians/reviews/` cover the retired posture framing only.
